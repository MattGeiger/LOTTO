# LOTTO v1.30 Planned Features

**Status:** Draft for product review  
**Target:** v1.30.0  
**Focus:** A small, durable expansion of the Arcade game catalog

## Decision Context

LOTTO's Arcade keeps waiting guests entertained without competing with the
queue. Snake and Brick Mayhem are the production games today. Day of the Dead
remains in the repository but is intentionally hidden in production while its
content and presentation are reconsidered.

The next game should fit the product rather than simply imitate a famous title:

- one clear control concept on a phone or an iPad;
- a round that is satisfying in roughly two to five minutes;
- immediate pause when a tracked ticket is called;
- no network dependency during play;
- deterministic pure game logic that can be tested outside React;
- original LOTTO names, copy, artwork, sound, and code;
- usable on the iOS/iPadOS 15 support floor;
- localizable without placing long sentences inside the playfield; and
- compatible with reduced motion, keyboard input, and touch input.

## Public-Domain and IP Boundary

"Public-domain game" is useful shorthand, but it is not the complete legal
test. The U.S. Copyright Office explains that ideas, systems, processes, and
methods of operation are not protected by copyright, while a particular
written, graphic, or artistic expression of them may be. The USPTO separately
explains that names, symbols, designs, and other source-identifying presentation
may function as trademarks.

For LOTTO, that means every game must use independently written code and rules
copy, original art and audio, and an original title and visual identity. Before
shipping, search the chosen title and presentation for trademark conflicts.
This planning document is not a legal opinion.

Primary references:

- [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 33 — Works Not Protected by Copyright](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf)
- [USPTO — What is a trademark?](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/what-trademark)
- [British Museum — ancient rules tablet for the Game of Twenty Squares](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_Rm-III-6-b)
- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art — Senet and Twenty Squares](https://www.metmuseum.org/pt/perspectives/ancient-egypt-board-games)

## Candidates

### Small, touch-first games

| Working title | Foundation | Product fit | Main work or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paddle Rally** | Generic paddle-and-ball play | One slider or drag control; short rounds; can reuse Brick Mayhem's fixed-step canvas patterns without copying its level system | Must feel distinct from Brick Mayhem and use an original name and cabinet art |
| **Memory Grid** | Traditional matching-pairs game | Calm, turn-based, broadly accessible, easy to pause, and well suited to original pantry/community artwork | Needs a complete accessible non-visual description and keyboard focus model |
| **Four in a Row** | Traditional connection game | Simple tap targets, pass-and-play or small deterministic AI, no reflex requirement | Do not use protected commercial branding or a branded board presentation |
| **Quick Tap** | Generic timed target game | Very small engine and short sessions; touch-first | Pure reflex play may exclude some guests unless difficulty and untimed modes are strong |
| **Pantry Run** | Original collect-and-avoid concept formerly drafted as Pantry Time | Mission-aligned setting, familiar one-stick movement, and room for original WTH art | Large art and level-design scope; must establish its own geometry and presentation rather than imitate a commercial cabinet |

### Historic strategy and race games

| Working title | Foundation | Product fit | Main work or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Twenty Squares** | Royal Game of Ur / ancient Twenty Squares lineage | Distinctive history, compact board, turn-based play, and natural pause points | Requires clearly documented chosen rules, a simple opponent, and original board art |
| **Nine Men's Morris** | Traditional strategy game | Small state space, accessible board, and deterministic AI | Longer learning curve than current Arcade games |
| **Mancala** | Traditional sowing-game family | Touch-friendly pits, low animation cost, and strong pass-and-play potential | The family has many rule sets; LOTTO must name and document one precise variant |
| **Reversi** | Nineteenth-century disk-flipping game | Clear 8×8 board, moderate AI scope, no reflex requirement | Use the generic Reversi name and an original visual treatment; avoid third-party branding |
| **Checkers** | Traditional draughts family | Familiar and accessible to many users | Correct forced-capture rules, AI, and draw detection make it larger than it first appears |
| **Senet** | Ancient Egyptian race game | Strong educational identity and original visual possibilities | Surviving evidence does not define one universally accepted ruleset; present any version as a documented reconstruction |

### Familiar card and pencil-game forms

| Working title | Foundation | Product fit | Main work or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solitaire** | Traditional patience games | Single-player, pausable, familiar | Drag/drop, card art, move validation, and small-screen layout make it a larger project |
| **Noughts and Crosses** | Traditional 3×3 game | Smallest possible AI and excellent accessibility baseline | Too brief to headline a release unless paired with another game or variant |
| **Dots and Boxes** | Traditional pencil-and-paper game | Simple tap interaction and pass-and-play | Needs a useful opponent and careful small-screen hit targets |

## Candidates to Exclude From This Track

- A falling-tetromino game with the familiar commercial board proportions,
  pieces, colors, or presentation.
- A pellet maze using recognizable characters, maze geometry, sounds, or
  cabinet presentation from a commercial franchise.
- A road-crossing, fixed-shooter, or space-rock game that preserves a famous
  title, character silhouette, enemy formation, sound, or screen composition.
- Any implementation described as a "clone" in product documentation.

Those mechanics may be generic at a high level, but reproducing recognizable
expression or branding is unnecessary risk when LOTTO can make something
original.

## Proposed v1.30 Scope

The release should choose **one** primary game after a small playable prototype,
not commit to the entire candidate list. The first prototype set should cover
three different interaction needs:

1. **Paddle Rally** — validates a minimal reflex game and maximum reuse of the
   existing canvas loop.
2. **Memory Grid** — validates a calm, accessible, non-reflex game.
3. **Twenty Squares** — validates a turn-based historic game with a lightweight
   opponent.

Product review should select the game whose prototype is clearest on the
smallest supported phone and most enjoyable in a three-minute waiting-room
session. The remaining prototypes should not ship as unfinished menu entries.

## Required Architecture

- Route: `src/app/(arcade)/arcade/<game>/page.tsx`.
- Pure engine: `src/arcade/game/<game>/{types,constants,engine}.ts`.
- Renderer and assets stay under the game's Arcade feature directory.
- Gameplay remains independent from raffle state and write APIs.
- The shared Arcade shell owns language, theme, queue banner, ticket-called
  pause, installed-app safe area, and pull-to-refresh integration.
- Appearance may style page chrome and controls, never game-state colors whose
  meaning must remain stable.
- High scores, if appropriate, use the existing per-game/per-difficulty Arcade
  store and never the core LOTTO database.

## Acceptance Gates

- Independent engine tests cover every transition, collision or legal move,
  scoring rule, pause/resume path, and deterministic random seam.
- Component tests cover start, pause, restart, ticket-called interruption,
  keyboard input, and touch input.
- All user-facing copy is present in every ready built-in locale and supports
  dynamic translation through the existing catalog.
- The game is playable at iPhone portrait, iPad portrait, and desktop sizes.
- Standalone mode keeps controls 32 px clear of the home indicator.
- Reduced motion does not remove information needed to play.
- Production build, legacy syntax scan, hydration smoke, iOS 15 simulator, and
  real minimum-device validation pass before promotion.
- README screenshots and `docs/user-guides/07-arcade.md` update when the chosen
  game ships; candidates do not appear as released features.

## Relationship to Older Plans

`docs/GAME.md` remains the Arcade separation and implementation record.
`docs/V2.0_PLANNED_FEATURES.md` contains an older "Pantry Time" concept. That
concept is now one creative direction to evaluate, not permission to copy a
commercial game's expression and not a reason to bypass the v1.30 prototype and
IP review gates above.
